# Guarana Suite — Canonical Bibliography

Single source list for the whole suite. Feature specs cite entries from here.

## Papers
- Yao, S. et al. (2022). *ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting in Language Models.* arXiv:2210.03629. — Inner-loop shape: interleaved Thought → Act → Observation.
- Shinn, N. et al. (2023). *Reflexion: Language Agents with Verbal Reinforcement Learning.* arXiv:2303.11366. — Reflection, episodic memory, failure feedback persisted between runs.
- Madaan, A. et al. (2023). *Self-Refine: Iterative Refinement with Self-Feedback.* arXiv:2303.17651. — Limits of unguided self-correction; motivates hard caps and independent verification over retry.
